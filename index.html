Atrium VR — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Atrium VR is a media player for PICO headsets, made by AtriumVR (contact: thebannables@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: nothing leaves your headset except the requests you ask it to make to your own media sources.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your headset, inside the app's private storage. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

  • The sources you add: server addresses, share names, folder paths and user names for headset storage, network shares (SMB), FTP, DLNA, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, XBVR and GraphQL library servers.
  • Passwords and access tokens for those sources. They are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore of your headset and never leave it, except to sign in to the source they belong to.
  • Your settings and viewing history: picture settings, the room you chose, window positions, and resume positions of videos from file sources.
  • Web browser data: the pages you open in the built-in browser keep their cookies and site storage on the headset, like any browser, so you stay signed in to sites. The app remembers the last page you opened.
  • A random app identifier (a UUID created on first start). It is sent only to Plex, Jellyfin and Emby servers you add, which use it to tell your devices apart. It is not linked to you or your headset in any other way.

Who the app talks to

  • Your own sources. The app connects only to the servers, shares and devices you add, on your local network, to list and play your media and — where the source supports it — to report watch progress back to it.
  • plex.tv, only when you choose "Link with plex.tv": to get a sign-in code and the list of your Plex servers. This is covered by Plex's own privacy policy.
  • Your local network, when you search for DLNA servers (a standard SSDP discovery message).
  • Websites you open in the built-in browser, as with any browser. For each site you open, the app sends that same site one extra request to see whether it is a video library server it can add for you (it asks the site's own address, nothing is sent anywhere else). Videos you send from a page to the player are fetched from their site, with that site's cookies.
  • Intiface Central, only if you switch on "Sync scripts to a device" and connect: a local WebSocket connection on your own network.

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to reach your media sources on your network.
  • Storage (read): only if you add "This headset" as a source, to list and play videos stored on the headset. The app asks for it at that moment.
  • Hand tracking: to let you control the app with your hands. Hand data is processed on the headset and not stored.

Children

Atrium VR plays media from sources you control and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
